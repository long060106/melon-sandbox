How to play

Sandbox

  • Pick something in the spawn menu, then click the world to place it. Right-click or Esc stops placing.
  • Drag: grab and throw anything. Double-click a person to control them.
  • Control: click a person to take control.
  • Delete / Pin / Boom / Heal do what they say. Keys 1–6 switch tools.
  • Space pause (when not controlling) · Z slow-mo · H help

Controlling a character

  • A/D or arrows: walk · W/Space: jump
  • J K L: Stand abilities (shown on the action pad)
  • P: strike a JoJo pose · Esc: let go

JoJo tips

  • Hits landed in stopped time are stored and all land at once when time resumes.
  • 26 Stands from Parts 3–8. Each one's J K L abilities are named on the action pad.
  • The World: press K again during your time stop to drop a Road Roller.
  • Killer Queen: K turns what you touch into a bomb. Press K again to detonate it.
  • Crazy Diamond: K puts nearby people back together, even the dead and the erased.
  • Made in Heaven speeds up time for everyone, and its user most of all. Gold Experience Requiem sends attackers back to zero.
  • Throw the Stand Arrow at a civilian to awaken a random Stand.
